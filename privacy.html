Privacy policy

Your commitment stays on your phone.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Short version: Rafael has no account, no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect, sell or share your data. What you block, your schedules and your PIN stay on your device.

Who we are

Rafael is made by Medit USA LTD ("we"). This policy covers the Rafael Android app and this website.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your lists: the apps, websites and phone numbers you choose to block or keep reachable.
  • Your commitment: when it starts and ends, and any per-item schedules.
  • Your setup PIN: never stored as-is. Rafael keeps only a salted, one-way hash of it.

All of this is saved only on your phone. We have no server that receives it, and we can't see it.

Permissions, and why Rafael asks for them

  • Display over other apps and usage access: to notice when a blocked app opens and cover it with Rafael's screen.
  • Accessibility (optional, "faster blocking"): used only to detect which app is in front so blocking can be instant. Rafael doesn't read what you type or what's on your screen.
  • VPN: Rafael runs a local filter on your phone that checks website names (DNS) against your block list. It is not a remote VPN, and your browsing isn't sent to us. Allowed lookups go to your network's normal DNS server, or a public one (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google 8.8.8.8) if none is available. Rafael doesn't keep a history of the sites you visit.
  • Call screening: to reject calls from numbers you blocked. Rafael never blocks emergency calls.
  • Contacts picker (optional): only the number you pick is saved, on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show that protection is on. Block notices don't include site names.

The only network request Rafael makes itself

To stop the commitment being shortened by changing the phone's clock, Rafael may check the current time from Google's connectivity check address (gstatic.com). No personal information is sent with that request.

This website

userafael.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. The download button asks GitHub which release is newest. If you email us, we use your message only to reply. If you share a story, we publish it only with your permission, and you can stay anonymous.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your phone, uninstalling Rafael after your commitment ends deletes it all.

Children

Rafael isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
